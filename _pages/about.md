I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024 - Present) under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. Before that, I completed the B.Eng. program (2020 - 2024) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, graduating in June 2024, where Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
